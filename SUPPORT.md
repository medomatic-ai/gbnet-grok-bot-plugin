# Support

The sole public contact for product support, security reports, and Marketplace questions is [support@gbnet.ai](mailto:support@gbnet.ai). Do not send credentials, OAuth tokens, connection codes, route credentials, webhook keys, verification nonces, Partner Content, or private Bot conversations.
